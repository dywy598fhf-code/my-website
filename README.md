# my-website

Kevin Nold's personal website.

## What's in here

- **`index.html`**: the whole website, in one file. It has three styles you can flip between with the dark switcher at the bottom of the page:
  1. **Calm**: minimal, light and quiet.
  2. **Glow**: dark, with soft coloured lights and glassy cards.
  3. **Bold**: big magazine-style type on warm paper.
- **`.claude/skills/`**: Emil Kowalski's design skills, which Claude uses to make things look and feel good.

## How to look at it

- **On the web:** once GitHub Pages is turned on, the site lives at https://dywy598fhf-code.github.io/my-website/
- **On your computer:** download `index.html` and double-click it. It opens in your browser.

Switch styles by clicking the switcher, or pressing `1`, `2`, `3` (or the arrow keys). Press `R` to replay the entrance animation.

## How to change the words

Open `index.html` and look for the part that says **YOUR CONTENT**. Change the text between the quotes and every style updates at once.
